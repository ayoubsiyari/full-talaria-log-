## Nuance on hazard #1 (do not mis-fix)

B-W16 brief §8a **accepts** that brand-new sessions stay `'unhydrated'` and rely on **hot autosave** to land the first journal. Blanket-extending B-W16 onto every hot journal write would close the wipe path and **break that accepted trade**.

The two arms that today look identical to `order-manager.js`:

| Arm | Meaning | Safe hot journal? |
|---|---|---|
| `!res.ok` → local-backup | We do not know | **No** — suppress |
| `res.ok` + null state → local-backup | Server said nothing (brand-new) | **Yes** — required |

Distinguishing them requires `chart.js` to pass a distinct hydrate reason. That is Manager A's territory (brief §8a already named it). Until then, Director choices on B-0126 are:

1. **A ships distinct reasons** + B gates hot journal only on the "we do not know" arm (same kill-switch), or
2. **Written residual acceptance** that failed-GET can still wipe via hot through canary.

Option "block all hot journal writes" is **withdrawn** — it contradicts the ratified brand-new-session trade.
