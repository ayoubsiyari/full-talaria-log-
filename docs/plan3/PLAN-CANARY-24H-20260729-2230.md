## The bar

- Zero tickets in `unknown`. Every row in the ledger says fixed or open, with a
  commit and a gate behind every fixed.
- No leak: multichart open/close cycles do not grow the heap floor without bound.
- Smooth: continuous paint on every panel, and the CPU ceiling brought down from
  137% of a core.
- Every remaining open issue labelled blocking or disclosed, by name, in writing.

**One honest constraint, stated once so nobody plans around a fiction.** 144
tickets cannot all be closed in 24 hours, and a canary that claims otherwise is a
canary that surprises the PO in front of users. Zero UNKNOWN is achievable and is
the bar. Zero OPEN is not. The difference is carried by the disclosure, and the
disclosure is built from D's labels, not from anyone's optimism.

## Standing orders for the next 24 hours

**KILL ON SIGHT.** A diagnosis that does not land a flagged fix in the same packet
is an incomplete packet. No more read-only reports that end at a recommendation.
Name it, cut it, flag it, gate it — one packet.

**Every cut ships behind a kill-switch.** FLAG-01, FLAG-02 and FLAG-03 apply in
full: testable against ABSENT, flippable without reload, and verified in the OFF
state against a working-product assertion. A switch is what lets us cut fast
without a debate, and the debate is what has been costing us days.

**Shoot suspects simultaneously, not in sequence.** Where several candidates
survive a measurement, cut all of them behind separate flags and let the PO's
instrument decide which mattered. Serial elimination is how a week disappeared.

**Nothing is declared dead by argument.** DECL-01 stands: an instrument says so,
or the PO says so. Both stay binding at speed — speed is the reason they exist,
not an exception to them.

**Escalate in one line, immediately.** If you are blocked, one line to the
Director naming the blocker and who unblocks it. Do not spend an hour writing up
why you are stuck. The PO is awake and I can rule in minutes.
