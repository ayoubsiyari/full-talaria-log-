## 4. The three things that can still cost us the deadline

**Gate 9 is the real risk.** We do not know what retains the orphaned engines. **Our stated mechanism was wrong** — I claimed a strong Map, both registries are WeakMaps, and A refuted it independently. **If the true retainer turns out to require a structural change rather than a null and a delete, the memory story changes shape late.** Everything else on this list is bounded work; this one is an unknown.

**Gate 15 is undecided and it is the PO's call, not mine.** The spikes have never been reproduced in two recordings. **I will not hold canary for a defect we cannot reproduce, and I will not describe it as fixed either.** The honest options are to disclose it as an observed-but-uncharacterised issue, or to hold. **A decision is needed before gate 14 can be finalised, because M7's wording depends on it.**

**And the pattern that has cost us most today: new discoveries.** The visibility defect was found at 17:35 having been present the whole time. **Nothing in this estimate accounts for the next one of those**, and there is no honest way to budget for it beyond the slack the numbers already show.
